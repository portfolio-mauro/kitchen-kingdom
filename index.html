The Story

The Kiwi King is dead! Very sad, obviously! But this is good news for you since the Kitchen Kingdom is in desperate need of a new king or queen. The first Hero to claim the ice crown will be the new ruler of the kitchen! But how is up to you! Are you


  1. …actually traveling through the whole Kitchen to retrieve the Ice Crown from the Freezer (aka land exactly on the finish space)…
  2. …or simply try to collect 12 H Ice Cubes to have a fake crown made?

Either way, you have to act fast, before someone else claims the crown!

Strategic Multi-Purpose A Action Cards

Which cards are you sacrificing to move forward or backward, and which cards do you want to keep to improve your gameplay or I Attack opponents?

How Good Is Your Aim?

Try to hit the centre of the box with your underhand throw to get bonuses added to your rolls when I Attacking players or determining the outcome of an B Event! G Defending an I Attack? Try to hit their dice to change their roll and it's box bonuses!

Game-Changing B Events Every Round

Mini-games, permanent rule changes, and unpredictable twists keep every game fresh and exciting.

f Upgrade Your Hero

Spend H Ice Cubes to permanently (or until they're stolen 😜) improve your rolls or your pick-ups along the way!

Free For All or Tag Team Mode

Conquer the kitchen by yourself or team up in pairs. Only 2 players? No problem, but watch out for the Bad Bean, an independent third player trying to make their claim for the crown!
